Questions

Is it a real person?

No. It’s an SI agent, what most people still call AI, and it says so at the start of every call. When something needs a person, like an emergency, it reaches you.

What does “SI” mean?

Super Intelligence. Since September 2026, the US government has used “SI” for what most people call AI.

Do I need a new phone number?

No. You forward your calls to us, all the time or only when you can’t pick up. Your number stays yours.

What does it cost?

Simple monthly pricing, charged per property for landlords. Early-access customers get founding pricing, and we’ll share the details when we invite you.
